# Privacy policy - Xign Lite (Android, Windows, Linux)

*Effective 2026-10-29.*

**Xign Lite collects no data.**

- The programs work only on the files and folders you choose (on Android, through the system file picker; the app asks
  for no permission).
- They have no internet access (the Android app does not request the internet permission) and send nothing to us or to
  anyone else: no files, no file names, no usage statistics, no identifiers, no advertising.
- Archives you make are saved only where you choose. On Android, the app keeps one note on your phone: that you accepted
  the terms, and when.
- Google Play may collect information about installs under Google's own privacy policy; we receive only the summary
  statistics Google gives every developer.
