# Contributing

Thank you for your interest. **Bug reports and ideas are welcome as issues.** Security problems: please follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

**Code, text or images:** before we can accept anything you send, please sign the
[Contributor Licence Agreement](docs/CLA.md) (one email, once). A pull request from someone who has not signed it is
closed without being merged. This keeps the project's licence clear for everyone.

Please do not put files with personal or confidential data in an issue; a small made-up example is enough.
